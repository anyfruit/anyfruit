## 🌟 Featured project — Planfect

**An AI day planner you talk to.** Tell it your plans by text or voice and it builds your day around
your routine, commute, and habits — schedules everything, flags conflicts, sends reminders, and shows
what's next on a home-screen widget. Designed and built end-to-end *(App Store release in progress)*.
