## 🚀 Featured products — all live, built solo

### 🔋 Policy Intel — energy-storage policy intelligence
<a href="https://policy-intel-production.up.railway.app"><img src="https://kejing-yan-portfolio-production.up.railway.app/assets/policy-intel.jpg" width="560" alt="Policy Intel dashboard" /></a>

China's energy-storage policy is scattered across 30+ provincial sites and a fog of industry media.
Policy Intel auto-discovers and scrapes official sources, runs **every document through a Claude
relevance / authenticity gate**, and serves a searchable dashboard — province heat maps, trend
analysis, version diffs, and weekly digests. **680+ policies · 30+ provinces · daily refresh.**

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `React` · `Claude (tool-use)` · `Brave Search` · `SQLite` · `Docker`
&nbsp;→&nbsp; **[Live dashboard ↗](https://policy-intel-production.up.railway.app)**

### 🗓️ Planfect — an AI day planner you talk to
<p>
  <img src="https://kejing-yan-portfolio-production.up.railway.app/assets/planfect.jpg" width="198" alt="Planfect — conversational planning" />
  <img src="https://kejing-yan-portfolio-production.up.railway.app/assets/planfect-schedule.jpg" width="198" alt="Planfect — day schedule" />
  <img src="https://kejing-yan-portfolio-production.up.railway.app/assets/planfect-analytics.jpg" width="198" alt="Planfect — time insights" />
</p>

Say one casual sentence — even by voice — and Planfect builds a realistic day around your routine,
real commute times, and habits, then writes it to your calendar. An **agentic tool-calling loop over
OpenAI** reasons about your schedule, real travel times come from Google Maps, and it's fully
bilingual (EN / 中文). iOS app *(App Store release in progress)*.

`Swift / SwiftUI` · `WidgetKit` · `Deno / TypeScript` · `Supabase (Postgres + RLS)` · `OpenAI` · `Google Maps`
&nbsp;→&nbsp; **[Case study ↗](https://planfect-support-production.up.railway.app/showcase)**

### 🅿️ Grab-a-Spot (一起抢车位) — social parking mini-game
<a href="https://grabaspot.up.railway.app"><img src="https://kejing-yan-portfolio-production.up.railway.app/assets/grabaspot.jpg" width="200" alt="Grab-a-Spot game" /></a>

A nostalgic QQ-style parking game rebuilt as a **production product** — park in friends' lots to earn
coins, collect fees, issue tickets, and prank each other. Runs as a **WeChat mini-game and a web app
on one Node backend**, with a shared pure-function `game-core` so server and client economics never
drift. **45+ API endpoints · 25+ gameplay modules · dual platform.**

`Node.js` · `Express` · `SQLite` · `WeChat Mini-Game` · `Canvas` · `Docker`
&nbsp;→&nbsp; **[Play it ↗](https://grabaspot.up.railway.app)**
